Featured project

Mossaratus

Mossaratus is one of the university projects I’m most proud of, both for the game and the animated trailer we made.

It’s a shooter where you fight waves of enemies as a six-armed robot. You buy different guns between rounds, and when you fire on one side, all the guns on that side go off together.

  • Team: Luís Antônio de Albuquerque Hollanda, Ernests Malnacs , Edina Depner, Tycho Heijmans
  • Role: I built the core of the shop system, which Ernests later refined, and designed and implemented the final boss and its attacks.
  • Tools: Unity and Blender

Time to Sleep

An installation that takes you from the habits of a day into a night of sleep.

Visitors entered daily habits at physical stations, made a sleep gesture, and explored a research-informed representation of the next morning through light, sound and a memory challenge.

My role: I processed sleep data and research, then worked with the team to bring those values into the installation.

Team: 14-person interdisciplinary team

Tools: Python, regression and decision-tree modelling, ESP32/Arduino

Dynamic Aquarium

A living aquarium where fish of different sizes move together as a school while keeping their own character.

My role: I made the fish code and tuned the flocking behaviour, and built the current, rocks, sand and boundaries.

Team: Luís Antônio de Albuquerque Hollanda, Tzuling Lin

Tools: Processing

Leaving a Trace

An interactive installation about the damage people can do to the environment, and how resilient nature can be. Visitors place and remove factories to change the landscape.

My role: I tuned how the landscape spreads and recovers, and worked on the interactive visuals and atmospheric audio.

Team: Luís Antônio de Albuquerque Hollanda, Felix A. Smelik, Amina I. Tatieva, Ebben M. R. van Reen, Eva Pos, Yanara P.C. Hoogwoud

Tools: Processing, ESP32/Arduino, projection, sound, fabrication
