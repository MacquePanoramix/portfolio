A look inside

Fish that move together

We made an aquarium where fish of different sizes and colours move through the same space. I worked on the fish code, adapting the jointed animation credited below and building the behaviours that guide the fish through the water.

I made and tuned the flocking algorithm, combining cohesion, separation and alignment with attraction to food and avoidance of the rocks, walls and floor. Together these behaviours shape the movement of the group.

A closer look at the animation

Following the head

In the Processing sketch, each fish has a spine of twelve joints. Each joint follows the one in front, staying a fixed distance away. Its angle is limited to 22.5° from the previous joint, so a turn travels down the body instead of snapping the whole fish around.

Guide the fish, then watch the rest of its body follow.

Use the arrow keys or drag the slider. The angle points above or below the rightward direction.

Motion starts paused. You can change the direction and inspect the pose without playing it.

See the original body update

For each joint after the head, the sketch finds the angle to the joint ahead, constrains it, and places the joint one link length behind.

for (int i = 1; i < spine.joints.size(); i++) {
  float curAngle = PVector.sub(spine.joints.get(i - 1), spine.joints.get(i)).heading();
  spine.angles.set(i, constrainAngle(curAngle, spine.angles.get(i - 1), spine.angleConstraint));
  spine.joints.set(i, PVector.sub(spine.joints.get(i - 1), PVector.fromAngle(spine.angles.get(i)).setMag(spine.linkSize)));
}

How the original sketch guides the head

The full aquarium calculates cohesion, separation, alignment, avoidance and food attraction for each fish. It combines these as weighted acceleration; the fish's orientation also uses alignment and its current velocity. The update then moves the head and asks the spine to follow.

updateAcceleration();
updateOrientation();
velocity.add(acceleration);
pos.add(velocity);
this.fish.spine.joints.set(0, this.pos);
this.fish.spine.angles.set(0, this.direction);
this.fish.updateBody();

In the study above, the slider takes the place of that steering so the spine can be examined on its own. Read Creature.pde · Read Fish_Flocks.pde

Beyond one fish

How the fish move together

In the original sketch the head is guided by several weighted steering behaviours. Their combination changes from moment to moment as the fish and their surroundings move.

↗ Cohesion
Move towards nearby fish.
↔ Separation
Keep space from close neighbours.
→ Alignment
Steer with the group’s direction.
✦ Food & obstacles
Approach food while avoiding rocks and the aquarium boundaries.

Making the water feel alive

I made the rocks, sand and boundaries, and a water current that follows the fish group's average velocity with a little smoothing. Its horizontal force moves the seaweed.

Tzuling created the bubbles, lighting, music and food, and handled the day and night modes. Tzuling also made the glowing particles that rise through the water at night.
